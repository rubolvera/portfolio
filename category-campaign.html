Case File

RPS Signature Programs Category Campaign

Project Objective

To distinguish RPS's program administrator capabilities and offerings from other business units within RPS, competing effectively against niche-based program administrators.

Audience

Primary: Independent insurance agents nationwide.

Marketing Strategy

Developed and launched a comprehensive category brand campaign for RPS Signature Programs, creating a distinct category message and visual identity that reflected the energy, enthusiasm, and approachability of the RPS culture in a bold, modern style. The campaign was designed to clearly communicate the brand promise, positioning, and key differentiators.

To amplify visibility, executed a high-impact media strategy across leading insurance trade publications — leveraging both print and digital formats. Through optimized placements and frequency-focused tactics, the campaign aimed to drive awareness and deepen engagement among agents and brokers.
