Case File

RPS Signature Programs Paid Media Campaign

IMCA — Bronze Award
Project Objective

To establish RPS Signature Programs as a leading brand in Program Administration by boosting awareness and differentiating from competitors.

Audience & Timing

Primary: Independent insurance agents nationwide.

The campaign launched heavily in March across print and digital media to maximize exposure in programs editorial features, continuing into May and slowly tapering off into the beginning of December. Digital placements were optimized throughout the campaign based on real-time performance measures, all leading to a campaign landing page for additional engagement.

Marketing Strategy

An awareness and lead-generation campaign focused on high-impact placements across insurance trade media, print, and digital formats — measuring and optimizing media tactics to drive awareness and engagement and increase the frequency with which agents and brokers interact with RPS Signature Programs.
