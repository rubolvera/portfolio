Case File

Public Entity Lead Gen Campaign

IMCA — Gold Award (2024)
Project Objective

To drive awareness of program availability from the same trusted carrier but under a new distribution partner, drive competitive positioning against the former Program Administrator, and ultimately re-capture public entity accounts back.

Audience

Primary: Appointed and active retail agent partners in AK, AZ, AR, CA, HI, IL, IN, LA, ME, MA, MI, MN, MS, MO, NJ, NM, NY, OK, PA, TX, UT, VA, WA, WV, and WY.

Secondary: Appointed and active retail agent partners nationwide, given the reach of RPS's social media channels.

The campaign launched heavily in March across print and digital media to maximize exposure in programs editorial features, continued into May to bring the new program to market across publishers, and included placements on LinkedIn. Digital placements were optimized throughout the campaign based on real-time performance measures, all leading to a campaign landing page for additional engagement.

Marketing Strategy

A lead generation campaign focused on high-impact placements across insurance trade media, print, and digital formats — measuring and optimizing media tactics to drive awareness and engagement and increase the frequency with which agents and brokers interact with the RPS Public Entity program. The campaign ran for five months, from March 2023 through July 2023.
