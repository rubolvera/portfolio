RPS Signature Programs Brand Creation

IMCA — Award of Excellence (2021)
Project Objective

To distinguish RPS Signature Programs, we sought to build clear and creative brand positioning, differentiated in voice and look from the primary competition within the Program Administration space, creating a recognizable identity for RPS Signature Programs and its producers and service teams.

After performing primary research in which we uncovered the true drivers of why independent agents and brokers reach out to — and choose — program administrators, plus a thorough competitive review, we produced an entirely new RPS Signature Programs brand message and creative execution. It directly answered the needs of our target audience while conveying the energy, enthusiasm, and approachability of the RPS Signature Programs culture with a modernized, bold style.

Brand Launch Campaign Creative

Sales collateral, signature-program category drip campaign creative, and program-specific campaigns.

Results

The brand advertising campaign was designed to build upon these brand fundamentals — showcasing the new RPS Signature Programs brand message and establishing our brand promise, positioning, and key takeaway.

A campaign focused on high-impact media placements across insurance trade media, print, and digital formats measured and optimized media tactics to drive awareness and engagement against the overall marketing objective: increasing the frequency with which agents and brokers interact with RPS.
